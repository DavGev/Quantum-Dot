The final numerical estimator was selected through the sequence summarized in
\cref{tab:validation}. These trials are not additional uncertainty samples;
they document why the complete correlation expression with only the
global rotation removed was adopted for production.

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\caption{Summary of the numerical validation sequence at $(a,c)=(5,1)$.}
\label{tab:validation}
\footnotesize
\begin{tabular}{
 >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.24\linewidth}
 >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.68\linewidth}}
\toprule
Trial & Main observation\\
\midrule
Reduced budget convergence &
The binding changed from $-0.00893$ to $+0.00240\ \Ry$ between
$10^6$ and $2\times10^6$ points; the worst independently integrated
biexciton identity spread was approximately $2.4\%$.\\
Dithered adaptive partitions &
Three runs with nonzero bisection dithering retained spreads of
approximately $1.9$--$2.7\%$; randomized partitioning did not remove the
problem.\\
Complete four-angle estimator &
All reflection and particle-exchange reductions were removed. The estimator
retained a redundant global rotation and displayed larger budget drift.\\
Global-rotation-fixed estimator &
One global angle was removed, while the other angles retained their full
ranges. This formulation agreed with the complete four-angle estimator within the
observed resolution at lower computational cost.\\
Staged ordinary minimization &
A low-budget optimization followed by high-budget readout remained vulnerable
to changes in candidate ordering.\\
Safeguarded pattern search &
Candidate moves were required to improve the energy at both $10^6$ and
$2\times10^6$ points and to exceed the observed budget drift. This became the
production protocol.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

For reproducibility, the machine-readable final table is distributed with the
manuscript source as the file
\begin{center}
\small\path{data/main-state-final-summary-Ry.csv}
\end{center}
The underlying project repository additionally retains the Wolfram Language
production drivers,
checkpointed WXF result stores, per-candidate and per-integral CSV records,
the plotting code, and the vector PDF figures. Each recorded
integral includes its value, internal error estimate, elapsed time, warning
text, and whether the point cap was reached.

The numerical history should be interpreted in the following hierarchy:
the final pattern-search stores define the optimized parameters; the final
summary CSV defines the plotted numerical values; and the earlier reduced,
dithered, and four-angle files are validation records rather than alternative
production estimates.
